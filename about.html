Quality Policy

Our 6-Stage Food Safety Protocol

Compliant with ISO 22000:2018 and FSSAI manufacturing regulations. No batch enters the distribution tunnel without rigorous laboratory sign-off.

1

Dairy Farm Intake Testing

Raw whole milk tankers are screened for antibiotic residues, aflatoxin, fat percentage, and SNF before offloading.

2

HTST Pasteurisation

High-Temperature Short-Time pasteurisation at 84°C eliminates all pathogenic bacteria while protecting dairy flavour notes.

3

Two-Stage Homogenisation

Pressure treatment at 2,500 PSI micronises butterfat globules into a uniform silky emulsion with zero ice graininess.

4

24-Hour Tank Ageing

Aged in refrigerated stainless holding tanks at 4°C to fully crystallise milk fat and hydrate natural stabilisers.

5

Continuous Dash Freezing

Injected with filtered sterile air under positive pressure for precise, repeatable commercial overrun control.

6

In-Line Metal & X-Ray Checks

Every filled tub passes automated check-weighers and high-sensitivity magnetic detectors prior to case packing.

In-House Microbiological Laboratory

Our plant operates a dedicated testing laboratory staffed by dairy technologists. We perform standard plate counts, coliform screening, yeast/mould cultures, and sensory organoleptic assessments for every single batch code.

Scale Progression

Growth Milestones

Our evolution into one of the region's leading dedicated B2B ice cream manufacturing facilities.

2018 — Inception & Mother Dairy Plant

Commenced commercial operations in Gujarat with an initial 5,000 litre/day continuous freezing line focused on HoReCa bulk catering tubs. [sample – client to confirm]

2020 — Reefer Fleet Expansion

Commissioned our proprietary captive fleet of 20 multi-temperature GPS-monitored refrigerated trucks, eliminating third-party transporter melt risks. [sample – client to confirm]

2022 — Automated Tub & Cone Line

Invested in automated Italian filling equipment expanding output to 15,000 litres/day. Introduced retail family tubs and crown waffle cones. [sample – client to confirm]

2024 — High-Bay Cold Storage & Multi-Hub Grid

Inaugurated 1,200 pallet blast freeze facility at -25°C and established direct distribution hubs across Mumbai, Delhi NCR, and Bengaluru. Output exceeded 20,000 L/day. [sample – client to confirm]

Present — National B2B Partner

Supplying over 35 cities with 600,000 litres monthly capacity, supporting supermarket private labels, leading hotel chains, and event caterers nationwide. [sample – client to confirm]
