Buyer Clarifications

Commercial Procurement FAQ

Key answers covering order minimums, delivery radius, certifications, and payment terms.

What is your Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ)? +
Our standard initial order minimum is 500 Litres for regional hub deliveries and 2,000 Litres for dedicated full-truckload (FTL) routes. We accommodate split-SKU assortments across catering tubs, cups, and cones within the same order. [sample – client to confirm]
What lead time do you require from PO to delivery? +
For active contract partners within 300 km of our central plant, dispatches arrive within 24 to 48 hours. Interstate truckloads require 3 to 4 business days. Emergency banquet top-ups can be expedited from our nearest regional hub. [sample – client to confirm]
How do you guarantee -18°C cold chain during transit? +
All deliveries travel in insulated multi-temp reefer vehicles equipped with real-time GPS telemetry and digital temperature recorders. Consignees receive an automated temperature printout at the receiving dock prior to unloading.
Can you produce custom flavours or private label packaging? +
Yes. We provide contract co-packing and private-label formulation for retail supermarket chains and restaurant franchises ordering a minimum of 10,000 Litres per month. [sample – client to confirm]
What are your standard B2B commercial payment terms? +
New accounts operate on 50% advance / 50% on dock receipt for the first 3 billing cycles. Established distribution partners with approved credit evaluations qualify for 15 to 30 day revolving credit limits. [sample – client to confirm]
Do you provide sample tasting kits before signing? +
Yes. Verified commercial procurement managers and chefs can request a complimentary 6-flavour insulated sample kit delivered with dry ice directly to your office or test kitchen.
